Mukoko News · classification

Topics, interest categories, stories, tags — four layers, four jobs

Topics are the big buckets a reader navigates by. Interest categories are the platform's fixed 40. Stories are events that unfold over days. Tags are open and always will be. The platform has two of the four.

rev. 3 · 10 Sep 2026 63,830 articles 39,368 embedded measured on the live cluster
Correcting revision 2

I called engagement.tags = aiKeywords a defect and proposed resolving keywords against a controlled vocabulary. Wrong on both counts — tags are meant to be open, so writing the model's keywords there is the intended behaviour, not a bug.

What is actually wrong with tags is far smaller: the same array is stored twice, and it is never normalised, so AI researcher and ai researcher are two different tags today. Open is not the same as unnormalised.

The four layers

Topic

~12 · editorial · stable

The big buckets, as Google News, the BBC and the NYT use them. A topic maps to several interest categories, and a category can sit in several topics — a genuine many-to-many. This is the nav.

Politics · Business & Economy · Africa · Sport · Technology · Climate

Interest category

40 · platform-wide · fixed

engagement.interestCategories. Shared with lingo and the other products, immovable, and not a news concept — which is exactly why a news topic layer has to sit above them rather than replace them.

politics-governance world-news football ai-machine-learning

Story

many · event-shaped · has a lifespan

A thing that is happening. It begins, it runs, it ends. Articles attach to it over days or months, and a reader can come back and scroll the whole arc. Lives at /story/[slug], stored so history survives.

Zimbabwe presidential election · Dangote Refinery IPO · CAF Champions League 2026

Tag

open · never a set list

Whatever this article is about, in its own words. No vocabulary, no approval, no registry. Normalised to a slug so the same term from two articles matches — and nothing more.

peter-obi n1-9tn strait-of-hormuz nintendo-switch-2

Stories

A story is not a bucket and not a tag. It is a thing that happened, with a first article and eventually a last one, and its value is exactly the thing the product cannot do today: let a reader come back next week and scroll the whole arc.

How a story is born

Not from a list — nobody knows next month's stories. From the embeddings, which are already there and already unused for this. 39,368 of the last fortnight's articles carry a BGE-M3 vector, and the articles_vector_search index already filters on datePublished, so a k-NN query against a rolling window works today with no index change: one vector query per new article, in a pipeline that already spends one AI call per article.

A neighbourhood becomes a story when it is dense, spans several distinct sources, and persists across more than one day. That middle test does most of the quality work by itself — one outlet writing about a thing is an article, five outlets over two days is a story. It rejects the noise without a rule per failure mode: in the live extraction sample it discards GreenWef (a garbled organisation) and September 16, 2026 (a date the model typed as an EVENT), while passing the Dangote Refinery IPO, which four Nigerian outlets covered inside 48 hours.

Where each piece lives

The split follows your own documented rule, not convenience: if a key under engagement.* on an article is not an engagement concern, it belongs in its own domain. Topics are classification, so they are engagement. A developing news story is a news concept, so it is not.

Collection / fieldHoldsWritten by
engagement.interestCategories The 40. Platform-wide, immovable, untouched by any of this. nobody — fixed
engagement.topics new slug, name, categorySlugs[], sortOrder, isActive. Twelve rows, edited by hand, changed about as often as a masthead. staff
article.engagement.topics new Denormalised topic slugs — derived from the article's categories, not asked of the model. enrichment
news.stories new The story registry and its lifecycle. In news because a developing news story is a news concept — and because it keeps the pipeline from writing another domain. story job + staff
article.storySlugs new Top-level, not under engagement. — a story is not an engagement concern, and the rule is explicit about that. story job
article.engagement.tags Open tags. Keep — but normalise to slugs and stop storing the same array twice. enrichment
engagement.tags The 288 improvised definitions. Retire — tags need no registry. Note 67 are country names duplicating the places domain, which is a domain violation sitting in the taxonomy today. deprecate

Build order

Numbered because the dependencies are real — nothing downstream is safe before 1 and 2 land.

01
mukoko-news-pipeline · fundi-news-enrichment

Classify into the 40

Replace CATEGORY_ENUM's invented literals with the 40 read from engagement.interestCategories, offered to the model as its ten groupNames rather than a flat list. assembleCategories keeps its role as the closed gate. Normalise tags to slugs in the same change, and stop writing the keyword array twice.

Gate: a failed startup read falls back to a pinned copy of the 40, never an open set — an empty vocabulary lets every classification through.
02
mukoko-news-pipeline · fly-worker

Backfill the 13 deterministic slugs

A pure map, oldest-first in bounded batches — the shape country_backfill already proves. No network, no AI. The four open rows wait for their decisions.

03
engagement · seed data

Create the twelve topics

Twelve rows in engagement.topics, each listing its categorySlugs. Deriving an article's topics is then pure set intersection against categories it already has — no model call, and it backfills the whole corpus in one pass.

Gate: every one of the 40 must appear in at least one topic, or articles in it become unreachable from the nav.
04
mukoko-news

Topic nav and /topic/[slug]

The category bar becomes a topic bar — twelve buckets instead of a title-cased list derived from article slugs. /topic/[slug] stops being a regex search and becomes what it should always have been: the bucket page, with its categories as filters inside it.

05
mukoko-news-pipeline · fly-worker

Story detection — read-only first

k-NN each new article against a rolling 7-day window; apply the density, multi-source and persistence tests; write candidates to news.stories with status: 'candidate' and touch no article.

Gate: this is the one step with real unknowns — the similarity threshold and window are empirical. Run it for several days and read the candidates before it writes storySlugs to anything.
06
mukoko-news · mukoko-news-gateway

/story/[slug] and the promotion screen

The existing timeline component moves from /topic/[slug] to /story/[slug] — it was built for this and has been pointed at the wrong concept. Staff name, merge and feature stories from an admin screen over the candidates; engagement.followActions, a collection that exists and is empty, finally has something worth following.

Decisions still open

Is the twelve-topic set right?

It is my proposal from the corpus, not a given. Africa is the one no generic section list would include and the one most worth arguing about — on a Pan-African product it may deserve to be the masthead rather than the third row. Crime & Justice is also a judgement: 414 articles say it earns a bucket, most Western outlets say it does not.

Recommend: ship these twelve, rename freely — they are twelve rows in a collection, not a migration.

sports → football or other-sports? · 384

The 40 have no generic sports category. Splitting needs the model, so these get re-enriched rather than backfilled.

Recommend: backfill all to other-sports, let re-enrichment promote football over time. Imprecise, never wrong — and the Sport topic covers both, so the nav is right either way.

health → mental-health-wellness? · 226

The only health category in the 40 sits in the Lifestyle group and names mental health. A cholera outbreak is not lifestyle content — but the platform already filed hospital, disease and pandemic there.

Recommend: follow the existing placement. The Health topic hides the awkwardness from readers; raise the naming with whoever owns the 40.

lifestyle has no counterpart · 67

“Lifestyle” is a group in the 40, not a category. There is nothing to map it to.

Recommend: drop and re-enrich — the same reasoning that removed general.
